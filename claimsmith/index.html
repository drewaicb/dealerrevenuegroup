How ClaimSmith™ Works

You advertise.
We run the paperwork.

After a short onboarding, the deadlines, the vendor follow-up and the portal work sit with DRG. Your team is not out of the loop entirely — we come to you for approvals, portal access, and the occasional question only your store can answer — but it is our job to keep it moving, and you get a monthly report of where every claim stands.

1

We map your eligible spend

We review your advertising activity case by case against current program rules, so your team is not the one deciding what qualifies or defending it later.

2

We collect the documents

We gather invoices, scripts, tear sheets, and proofs of performance directly from your vendors — no chasing on your end.

3

We submit & track

Pre-approvals and claims are filed through the BMW/MINI dealer marketing portal (BrandMaster, now Papirfly), and every submission is tracked to payment.

4

We chase the denials

Denied claims get reworked and refiled inside the program’s resubmission window — the step that gets dropped when co-op is somebody’s ninth priority. You get monthly reporting and alerts before any fund expires.

What's Included

Built so the deadlines are not your team's to remember.

Case-by-case eligibility review

Pre-approval submissions

Deadlines tracked per program

Filing windows differ by program — BMW/MINI retail co-op runs 90 days from the ad date, Aftersales is shorter, and denial resubmission is shorter still. We track the window that applies to each claim rather than one blanket rule.

Direct vendor document collection

Monthly reporting & fund alerts

  • BMW or MINI dealers whose co-op sits with a marketing manager, office manager, or GM who already has a full-time role
