Daily Stack Support

Need help with the Daily Stack beta? Email andy@dealerrevenuegroup.com. Include your iPhone model and what you were doing when it went wrong; a screenshot helps.

Sign-in and password

Check your spam folder for the confirmation or reset email. Links expire, so request a new one from the sign-in screen if it no longer works. Still stuck? Email us from the address you signed up with.

Reminders not showing

Open iPhone Settings → Notifications → Daily Stack and turn on Allow Notifications. Focus modes can also hold reminders back. Then open Daily Stack once so it can reschedule them.

Photo entry

Photo entry is limited to invited beta accounts and a few scans a day. Manual entry always works. If the photo draft misreads a label, fix the fields before saving; nothing is saved until you tap Save.

Beta feedback

In TestFlight, take a screenshot in the app and tap Share Beta Feedback, or just email us. Small things count.

Export or delete your data

In the app, go to Profile → Export data or Delete account and data. Deleting removes your account, records, and label photos. Details are in the Privacy Policy.
