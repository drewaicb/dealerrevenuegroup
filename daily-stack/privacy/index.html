Daily Stack Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 10, 2026

This policy covers the Daily Stack private beta, operated by Dealer Revenue Group, LLC. Daily Stack helps adults organize their own supplements, schedules, reminders, and intake records. It does not provide medical advice, diagnosis, treatment, or dosage recommendations.

Information you provide

We store your account email, authentication records, supplement names and label details, schedules, dose-completion records, optional notes and check-ins, and any supplement-label photos you choose to upload. We also keep account preferences, beta-access allowances, and scan records needed to operate the app. This beta does not collect bloodwork, precise location, advertising identifiers, or payment-card information. Purchases are disabled.

How information is used

We use this information to sign you in, synchronize your private account, display your supplement routine, provide reminders you enable, create exports you request, and diagnose service failures. Device reminders are scheduled locally. Service providers may process network information, such as IP addresses and request timestamps, to deliver and secure the service.

We do not sell your information or use supplement records or photos for advertising. Your account records are not shared with other beta testers. You choose whether to share an exported supplement list; anyone you share it with can retain their copy.

Optional AI photo entry

Manual entry is available without sending photos to an AI provider. When an eligible invited account chooses AI photo entry and consents, the app uploads front and back label photos to private Supabase storage and sends those images and label-reading instructions through OpenRouter to an eligible inference provider running Qwen3 VL 8B Instruct. The AI request does not include your account email, notes, check-in history, or supplement schedule. Avoid including people, prescription labels, or other personal information in bottle photos.

Requests are configured to require zero-data-retention routing and to deny providers that collect request content. These routing controls are subject to the providers' service terms and do not delete the private copies in your Daily Stack account. We retain the extracted draft, scan status, model, token counts, and recorded processing cost for account exports, retries, usage limits, and service operation. AI results can be incorrect; compare the draft with the original label before saving.

Service providers

Supabase provides account authentication, database hosting, private photo storage, and server functions. OpenRouter and the selected inference provider process optional AI label requests. Expo provides app build and distribution services, and Apple provides TestFlight distribution and its testing feedback services. These providers operate under their own service and privacy terms. Daily Stack does not send your supplement database to Expo or Apple as part of building or distributing the app. If you submit feedback or screenshots through TestFlight, Apple processes what you choose to include.

Retention and deletion

We retain account data while your account is active. In Profile, you can export your account data or select Delete account and data. Account deletion removes your authentication account, associated app records, and uploaded label photos from active Daily Stack systems. Limited operational logs and provider backups may remain under the providers' retention practices and legal or security requirements. Deletion does not remove copies you previously exported or shared, or feedback separately submitted to Apple. Contact us if you need help with a deletion request.

Security and your choices

We use encrypted connections, authenticated sessions, private photo storage, and database rules that restrict records to their account owner. No service can guarantee absolute security. You can deny or revoke camera, photo-library, and notification access in your device settings. You can enter supplement details manually, choose whether to use AI photo entry, edit your records, and request help with access, correction, export, or deletion.

Audience and changes

This private beta is intended for adults in the United States and is not directed to children under 18. We may update this policy as the app changes and will update the effective date. Material changes will be communicated through the app or account contact details as appropriate.
