Questions

Frequently asked.

Is there a service that monitors what AI assistants say about my dealership?

Yes — Narrative Monitor by Dealer Revenue Group does exactly that. Every week we ask ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Perplexity, and Grok the questions your customers ask about your dealership (or any local business), score whether and how you're named versus your competitors, and email you a plain-English report with the fixes ranked by impact. Plans start at $299/month, and a free one-time audit shows you your score first.

How is this different from SEO?

SEO gets you ranked in a list of links. GEO (Generative Engine Optimization) gets you named in the answer when someone asks an AI assistant directly. The signals overlap but aren't the same — AI leans heavily on reviews, third-party sources, and how consistently the web describes you. Full explainer here.

Which AI assistants do you monitor?

ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Perplexity, and Grok (Pro). Claude, Gemini, and Perplexity are queried with live web search; ChatGPT and Grok reflect what the models already believe about your business — both views matter, because customers use both.

How often do reports arrive?

Weekly, with a monthly progress recap that graphs your visibility over time. It's scheduled monitoring, not a real-time alert feed — AI answers shift over weeks, and that's the cadence that catches it.

Do you make the fixes for us?

The report tells your team exactly what to do, in priority order — which reviews to ask for, what pages to publish, which Reddit threads to answer — plus 2 ready-to-shoot content briefs each week. Producing the content and collecting the reviews stays with you, so the results are authentically yours.

What's a custom question?

A question you write in your customers' words — "who has the best guitar lessons in Madison?" — that we put to the AI assistants on every run alongside the standard set. Standard includes 1; Pro includes 5.
