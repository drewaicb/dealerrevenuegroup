How Narrative Monitor Works

We ask. We score.
You get the fixes.

1

We ask what your customers ask

Every week we put the real questions — "best near me," pricing, reputation, your own custom questions — to ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Perplexity, and Grok, including live web-search results from Claude, Gemini, and Perplexity.

2

We score every answer

Were you named? Named first? Was a competitor recommended instead? Every response is scored into a 0–100% visibility score you can track week over week.

3

You get the report

A weekly email with the executive summary up top — your score, real AI quotes about your business, and prioritized action items — plus a monthly progress recap with graphs.

4

You fix what moves the score

Actions are ranked from what your own results show — the questions where you were left out, the competitors named instead, and the sources the assistants leaned on — plus 2 ready-to-shoot video/content briefs built from that week's findings. No AI company publishes its ranking formula, so we prioritise on observed answers and visible source evidence rather than claiming to know the recipe.

Questions

Frequently asked.

Is there a service that monitors what AI assistants say about my business?

Yes — Narrative Monitor by Dealer Revenue Group does exactly that, for dealerships and non-automotive businesses alike. Every week we ask ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Perplexity, and Grok the questions your customers ask, score whether and how you're named versus your competitors, and email you a plain-English report with actions ranked by what your own results suggest matters most. The paid subscription starts at $299/month. Separately, a free one-time audit shows you where you stand today, with no subscription.

How is this different from SEO?

SEO gets you ranked in a list of links. GEO (Generative Engine Optimization) gets you named in the answer when someone asks an AI assistant directly. The signals overlap but aren't the same — AI leans heavily on reviews, third-party sources, and how consistently the web describes you. Full explainer here.

Which AI assistants do you monitor?

ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Perplexity, and Grok (Pro). Claude, Gemini, and Perplexity are queried with live web search; ChatGPT and Grok reflect what the models already believe about your business — both views matter, because customers use both.

How often do reports arrive?

Weekly, with a monthly progress recap that graphs your visibility over time. It's scheduled monitoring, not a real-time alert feed — AI answers shift over weeks, and that's the cadence that catches it.

Do you make the fixes for us?

The report tells your team exactly what to do, in priority order — which reviews to ask for, what pages to publish, which Reddit threads to answer — plus 2 ready-to-shoot content briefs each week. Producing the content and collecting the reviews stays with you, so the results are authentically yours.

What's a custom question?

A question you write in your customers' words — "who has the best guitar lessons in Madison?" — that we put to the AI assistants on every run alongside the standard set. Standard includes 1; Pro includes 5.
