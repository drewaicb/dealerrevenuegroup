Why Co-op Goes Unclaimed

The money is earned. The paperwork is the problem.

1

Hard deadlines

Most programs require claims within 90 days of the ad date. Miss it and the reimbursement is gone — no appeal.

2

Detailed documentation

Itemized invoices, scripts, tear sheets, affidavits, screenshots — summary bills get denied, and vendors are slow to send the rest.

3

Denials nobody chases

Denied claims can usually be fixed and resubmitted within a short window — but only if someone owns that follow-up.

4

Nine other jobs

Whoever handles co-op at a dealership almost always has a full-time role already. Claims lose to whatever's urgent that day.

Common Questions

Co-op recovery, answered plainly.

How much co-op money do dealerships typically leave unclaimed?

Industry estimates put unclaimed automotive co-op in the billions each year. At the store level it's commonly thousands per month — not because the money isn't earned, but because claiming it is tedious and deadline-bound.

Why do co-op claims get denied?

Missing itemized invoices, missing proof of performance, non-compliant creative, late filing, or ineligible expenses. Most denials are fixable — programs typically allow ~30 days to resubmit — if someone actually reworks the claim.
