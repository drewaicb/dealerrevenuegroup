How Expense Tracker Works

You send one export.
Your report arrives in your inbox.

There is no software for your team to learn and nothing to connect to your DMS or accounting system. You export what you already can, we do the rest, and the report arrives monthly.

1

What you send

One account-detail export from your DMS after each month closes, a few minutes of work for accounting. Upload it in your DRG portal. You control exactly what you send: the AP detail, never customer data. Converting the export is our job.

2

What we clean up

Every posting line is matched to a clean vendor name and a category, then organized by brand, department and month — advertising and outside services kept separate rather than blended.

3

What we compare

Each month is set against the month before, and against the same month last year once we hold a comparable prior-year period — which we can load at signing rather than waiting a year to build it. Your co-op-eligible advertising spend is set beside what has actually been claimed on it.

4

What you get

An executive report in the inbox of everyone you name, every month. The full vendor detail behind every number is one click away in your DRG portal.

The Deliverable

What is actually in the report.

Built for the people who sign off on spend: GMs, owners, COOs and CFOs. Clarity on your expenses you have never had from your DMS, so nothing slips through because a manager recognized the vendor name on a bill.

Executive summary

The whole month on one screen: total advertising and OSS, the movement against last month and last year, and where the change came from.

Vendor detail — advertising

Every advertising vendor, by brand and department, by month, with the line-item detail behind each total.

Vendor detail — outside services

The same treatment for OSS, which is usually the half nobody at the GM level has ever seen itemized.

Co-op utilization

Advertising spend at co-op-eligible vendors, shown beside what has actually been claimed against it. It is a place to look, not a balance you are owed — eligibility, fund rules, deadlines and approvals all still decide what a claim is worth. Claimed and paid figures populate only for vendors DRG files for.

Year-over-year comparison

This year against last, by vendor and department — the view that turns "we spend a lot on that" into a defensible decision. It appears once a comparable prior-year period has been loaded — at signing, if you want the comparison from day one; month-over-month is there from the second month on.

Re-pull safe

If accounting re-issues a month, the corrected export replaces the old one instead of double-counting it. Sending the same month twice is not a problem.
