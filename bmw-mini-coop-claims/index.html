Common Questions

BMW & MINI co-op, answered plainly.

What's the deadline for BMW and MINI co-op claims?

Generally 90 days from the advertising date, with about 30 days to resubmit after a denial. Funds themselves also expire on program schedules — money that isn't tracked disappears quietly.

What documentation is required?

Itemized vendor invoices plus proof of performance for the media type: scripts and affidavits for broadcast, tear sheets for print, screenshots and reporting for digital. Summary invoices are a leading denial cause.

Can a third party submit claims for my store?

Yes — BMW and MINI retail co-op allows authorized third parties like DRG to prepare and submit through BrandMaster. Aftersales co-op must be dealer-submitted; we give you one-page instructions for that piece.

We're not a BMW or MINI store — can you still help?

Yes. ClaimSmith™ works across brand programs and portals (BrandMaster, Papirfly, and others). Start at co-op recovery for the brand-agnostic picture.
