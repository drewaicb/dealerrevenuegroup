Tugboat Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 14, 2026

This policy covers the Tugboat beta, operated by Dealer Revenue Group, LLC. Tugboat helps adults start tasks, get reminders, and leave on time. It does not provide medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment.

Your data stays on your phone

Tugboat has no accounts and no Tugboat servers. Your tasks, steps, lists, brain dumps, settings, and checklists are stored only on your iPhone. We cannot see them, and they are not sent to us. Reminders, alerts, the Lock Screen countdown, and the widget are created on your phone.

Tugboat does not include analytics, advertising, or tracking tools. It does not collect advertising identifiers, contacts, health data, or payment information.

Location (optional)

If you tap "Check with Apple Maps" when adding a trip, iOS asks for permission to use your location while the app is open. Your current location and the place name you typed are sent to Apple Maps to calculate travel time. Tugboat does not store your location, does not use it in the background, and does not send it to us. You can turn location access off in iOS Settings at any time.

Calendars (optional)

If you choose to connect a calendar, Tugboat reads and writes only the calendars and events you select, on your phone. Calendar data is not sent to us.

Sharing you start

Some features open the iOS share sheet or Messages, such as sending a "running behind" text or sharing tasks with an AI assistant you choose. Only the text you see is shared, and only when you tap to share it. Whoever you share it with can keep it.

Service providers

Apple provides TestFlight distribution, Apple Maps travel times, crash reports you choose to share, and TestFlight feedback. Expo provides app builds and app updates; when Tugboat checks for an update, Expo receives standard request information such as the app version, device platform, an anonymous install identifier, and network details like IP address. These providers operate under their own terms and privacy policies. If you send feedback or screenshots through TestFlight, Apple processes what you choose to include.

Deleting your data

Deleting Tugboat from your iPhone deletes all of its data. Individual tasks and lists can be removed in the app at any time.

Audience and changes

This beta is intended for adults in the United States and is not directed to children under 18. We may update this policy as the app changes and will update the effective date.
