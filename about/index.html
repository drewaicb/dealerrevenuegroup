About DRG

DRG (Dealer Revenue Group, LLC) is a southern Wisconsin company that helps businesses see their own numbers clearly and collect money they have already earned. Andy LaBerge founded it. We run three managed services, and you can take any one of them on its own.

What we do

  • Narrative Monitor asks ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Perplexity, and Grok the questions your customers ask. It scores whether and how each assistant names you against your competitors, then sends a plain-English monthly report with fixes ranked by impact. It works for any industry. Plans start at $299 per month.
  • Expense Tracker takes your existing account-detail exports and turns them into one branded monthly workbook of advertising and outside-services spend, broken out by vendor, department, and month. It starts at $99 per month.
  • ClaimSmith™ is fully managed advertising co-op claim recovery: we collect the documents, file the pre-approvals and claims, rework denials, and report what was paid. It is operational today for BMW and MINI co-op programs.

Who runs it

Andy LaBerge founded DRG after more than 20 years in retail operations, where he spent a lot of time on the same problems our services solve: co-op money left unclaimed, ad spend scattered across vendor invoices, and no clear view of how the business looked to customers. DRG turns those jobs into services with a flat monthly price. They are fully managed, so they save your team time instead of adding work.

How we work

Every service has a flat monthly fee. We never take a percentage of what we recover. There's no long-term contract. If a service isn't a fit for your business, we'll say so before you sign up.
