Expense Tracker

Expense control is critical to every dealership's success — but DMS reports and financial statements rarely give you the detail and clarity you need to act. They roll thousands of dollars into a handful of summary lines, and pulling them apart by vendor, department, and month means hours in spreadsheets every time you want a straight answer. Advertising you probably already have a feel for — those numbers cross your desk. Outside services are the blind spot: OSS bills get approved at the department level and rarely reach the GM at all, which is why that's so often where the opportunity is.

The Expense Tracker turns your existing exports into one clear picture of where the money goes — advertising and OSS by vendor, department, and month, with month-over-month and year-over-year trend built in, plus a co-op reconciliation that shows what you actually received against what you spent. No spreadsheets, no manual reconciliation — just the clarity to make decisions you can defend.

  • Advertising & OSS spend in one branded report — summary dashboard up front, full line-item detail behind it
  • Broken out by brand, vendor, department, and month, with month-over-month and year-over-year trends
  • Co-op received vs. utilization reconciliation — unclaimed balances flagged
  • Delivered monthly to your DRG portal, with a copy emailed to your team
  • Works from your existing reporting exports — no CRM or accounting integration required
  • Included with ClaimSmith™
