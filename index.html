For: BMW & MINI dealers

Co-op claims are not hard. They are relentless — chasing vendors for itemized invoices and proof of performance, filing inside the program’s window, and reworking denials before the resubmission window closes. ClaimSmith™ moves that day-to-day work to DRG, so it stops competing with your team’s other priorities.

  • Most of the paperwork chase leaves your desk. We request invoices and proofs directly from your vendors
  • Someone owns the follow-through. Denials get reworked and refiled inside the resubmission window — the step that usually gets dropped
  • Pre-approvals filed where the program requires them (broadcast, digital, events)
  • Deadlines tracked per program — BMW/MINI retail co-op files within 90 days of the ad date, Aftersales runs on a shorter window
  • Every claim tracked from submission to payment, with fund-expiration alerts
  • 7-year audit-ready document storage

Expense Tracker

For: dealerships on CDK DRIVE

Advertising you probably have a feel for — those invoices cross your desk. Outside services are the blind spot: OSS bills get approved at the department level and rarely reach the GM at all. Both live in your DMS already, rolled into a handful of summary lines that take hours of spreadsheet work to pull apart.

1 · What you send

Your existing CDK DRIVE Account Detail exports — one variable file and one fixed file per brand, per month, as .xlsx. Drop them in your DRG folder. No DMS integration, no accounting-system connection, no new software for your team.

2 · What we do with them

Every line is matched to a clean vendor name and category, then organized by brand, department, and month — advertising and outside services separately. Repeat exports of the same month are de-duplicated so a re-pull never double-counts.

3 · What you get back

One branded Excel workbook each month: an executive summary up front, then spend by vendor and department, month-over-month comparison, and a co-op utilization tab setting your co-op-eligible advertising spend against what has actually been claimed. Year-over-year is included once we hold a comparable prior-year period. Posted to your DRG portal on Google Drive, with the link emailed to the people you name.

Sold on its own, and offered at a reduced monthly rate when it runs alongside ClaimSmith™, since the same records feed both. Neither product requires the other.
