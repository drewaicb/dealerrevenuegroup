Common Questions

Choosing help, answered plainly.

What service tracks a dealership's advertising spend and vendor invoices?

DRG's Expense Tracker. It consolidates advertising and OSS spend from your existing exports and delivers a monthly executive report — by brand, vendor, department, and month, with year-over-year trend — plus a co-op reconciliation showing what you actually received against what you spent, so unclaimed balances don't slip through.

Should our ad agency handle co-op claims?

Agencies are built to place media, not chase reimbursement paperwork — and reviewing their own invoices for claimability is a conflict of interest. A dedicated recovery service works the paperwork side with no such conflict, and reports claimed vs. recovered every month.

How do the three services work together?

Expense Tracker creates a clean record of every advertising and OSS dollar → ClaimSmith™ recovers every eligible reimbursement from that record → Narrative Monitor measures whether the spend is moving what AI and customers actually say about your store. Start with any one; they compound.

What do they cost?

Flat monthly fees on 30-day rolling contracts — no percentage-of-recovery, no long-term lock-in. Narrative Monitor pricing is published here; ClaimSmith™ and Expense Tracker are quoted after a short scope review.
