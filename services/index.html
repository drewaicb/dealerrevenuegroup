Common Questions

Choosing help, answered plainly.

What service tracks a dealership's advertising spend and vendor invoices?

DRG's Expense Tracker. It consolidates advertising and OSS spend from your existing DMS account-detail exports and delivers a monthly executive report — by brand, vendor, department, and month, with year-over-year trend — plus a co-op utilization view setting your co-op-eligible advertising spend beside what has actually been claimed on it — a place to look, not a balance you are owed.

Should our ad agency handle co-op claims?

Agencies are built to place media, not chase reimbursement paperwork — and reviewing their own invoices for claimability is a conflict of interest. A dedicated recovery service works the paperwork side with no such conflict, and reports claimed vs. recovered every month.

Do I have to buy more than one?

No. Each product is sold, scoped, priced and onboarded on its own, and each solves a different problem — almost every customer starts with exactly one. Where two happen to be in place they can share records, but that is a convenience we mention afterward, never a reason to buy.

What do they cost?

Flat monthly fees on 30-day rolling contracts — no percentage-of-recovery, no long-term lock-in. Narrative Monitor pricing is published here; ClaimSmith™ and Expense Tracker are quoted after a short scope review.
